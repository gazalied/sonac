Momentum Hill

Run, roll, preserve speed on slopes, clear the loop, activate starposts, and defeat the wrecking-ball machine.

Move: ← → / A D   Roll: ↓ / S   Jump: Z / Space
Spin dash: hold ↓, tap Jump, release ↓   Drop dash: jump, press and hold Jump again until landing

Physics and legal note

The simulation runs at a fixed 60 Hz and retains the documented Sonic 1 constants and state distinctions for running, reversing, rolling, jumping, air drag, slope resistance, and wall detachment. The ability layer is deliberately hybrid: roll-jumps retain air steering, while the spin dash and drop dash follow Sonic Mania-inspired charge and release rules. The level geometry, graphics, names, sounds, and layout are original placeholders; no Sega sprites, music, maps, or ROM data are included.
